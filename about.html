Professional focus

My work sits at the intersection of enterprise Data & AI platforms, reliability engineering, and intelligent operational systems.

I focus on a recurring engineering challenge: how can enterprise data platforms move beyond detecting failures toward understanding them, learning from prior incidents, and safely supporting corrective action?

I describe that specialization as reliability intelligence: combining failure understanding, historical incident reuse, operational memory, AI-assisted diagnosis, and verification so data systems can recover more safely and consistently.

My technical work spans historical incident reuse, failure similarity and embeddings, incident-memory architectures, failure fingerprinting, LLM-assisted self-healing, and benchmarking approaches for reliable data systems.

I also contribute to the technical community through scholarly peer review, applied AI judging, technical program service, and practitioner-focused writing.
