A single professional narrative

My work sits at the intersection of enterprise Data & AI, large-scale data platforms, and intelligent autonomous systems.

Across industry and research, I focus on a recurring question: how can enterprise data platforms move beyond detecting failures toward understanding them, learning from prior incidents, and safely taking corrective action?

That question has led to work on historical incident reuse, failure similarity and embeddings, incident-memory architectures, failure fingerprinting, LLM-driven self-healing, and benchmarking approaches for reliable autonomous data systems.

In parallel, I contribute to the technical community through scholarly peer review, invitation-only AI hackathon judging, technical program leadership, and accessible technical writing.
