Industry impact

Enterprise Data & AI systems built for scale, reliability, and operational use.

This page focuses on the kinds of technical problems solved in enterprise environments without exposing confidential implementation details or inventing impact metrics.

Platform domains

From data infrastructure to AI-enabled operations.

Industry experience provides the operational context for the research agenda: large-scale systems, real failure modes, strict reliability needs, and increasingly AI-assisted workflows.

01 · Data platforms

Large-scale cloud & lakehouse architecture

Challenge — reliable ingestion, transformation, storage, and serving across distributed enterprise data environments.

Approach — architecture and engineering using Databricks, Apache Spark, Azure, Kafka, streaming, and lakehouse patterns.

Impact lens — throughput, reliability, recoverability, cost efficiency, data quality, and developer/operator productivity.

02 · Reliability

Operational intelligence for data systems

Challenge — recurring failures are repeatedly diagnosed from scratch when prior incident knowledge is fragmented.

Approach — observability, failure fingerprinting, incident reuse, memory layers, and retrieval of similar historical events.

Impact lens — faster diagnosis, reduced recurrence, safer remediation, and reusable operational knowledge.

03 · Enterprise AI

LLM and agentic-system integration

Challenge — probabilistic AI must operate inside systems that require deterministic controls and production reliability.

Approach — hybrid architectures combining retrieval, reasoning, policy, verification, guardrails, and human escalation.

Impact lens — useful automation without sacrificing traceability, control, or operational safety.
