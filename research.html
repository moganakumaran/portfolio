Research

Data & AI Engineering for reliable, intelligent systems.

My research examines how enterprise data platforms can evolve from observability-driven systems into memory-enabled, intelligent systems that can reason over operational evidence and support controlled self-healing.

Research thesis
Modern Data & AI platforms should not merely detect failures. They should learn from historical incidents, reason about recurrence, safely recommend remediation, and progressively evolve toward trustworthy autonomous operation.
